# About

## Mission

Build a welcoming, inclusive community that shares the joy of playing board and card games in person.

## Organization & Management

There is no legal entity that is the Asheville Board Game Club.

There is a group of co-organizers who have volunteered to guide the club in support of its mission. Currently, this group consists of these co-organizers:

- David Corbin
- Will Haskell
- Olivia B.
- West

## History

ABGC was founded in May of 2021 by a board game enthusiast and has continued with changing leadership over the years.
