# Meetups

- Attendance is free!
- Feel free to bring any games you want to play. Most gamers are happy to share the games they bring.
- We try to be very welcoming of new players, but sometimes everyone is already in a game, and you'll have to wait until something ends or other players show up.
- First time? Lots of us were nervous at our first meetup too. You're in good company.
